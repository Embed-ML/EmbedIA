# EmbedIA Authors and Contributors

EmbedIA is an open-source project developed within the framework of the **Instituto de Investigación en Informática LIDI (III-LIDI)**, School of Computer Science, National University of La Plata (UNLP), Argentina.

## Main Authors

- **César Estrebou** – Project Design, Main Development, Documentation
- **Marcos David Saavedra** – Project Design, Main Development, Documentation

## Academic Support

This project was made possible through the collaboration of faculty advisors and students participating in academic innovation programs at UNLP.
